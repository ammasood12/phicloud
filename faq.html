Ping Rate is technically based on the distance between your location and the server.

Green (Good) Blue Yellow Red (Timeout or no connection)

Ping rate does not define your actual Download/Upload speed.

Packages & Pricing

What is difference between packages?

  • Basic — 500GB/Month (for basic usage, social media etc.)
  • Super — 1000GB/Month 🔥 (for high-quality streaming)
  • Max — unlimited/Month (High speed, watch 4k videos without buffering, gaming, etc.)
  • Nano — 500GB permanent data, 100Mbps - no monthly reset, no device limit
  • Micro — 1000GB permanent data, 100Mbps - no monthly reset, no device limit
Note: You can either get Large Traffic/Data or High Speed, both can't be in the same package together.

These data limits are based on the usage (similar to using mobile data limit). Therefore if you use internet while being connected your package data will also be consumed based on your usage. This data limit will reset every month (except Nano & Micro which have permanent data).
