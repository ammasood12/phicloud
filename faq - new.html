What does "no reset" mean?

No reset means the data is permanent — you can use it until the data is over or your subscription expires. There is no monthly data reset for these packages (Nano and Micro).

For example, Micro gives 1500 GB for 6 months. You can use that 1500 GB at any pace until it runs out or the 6 months end.

Packages & Pricing

What is difference between packages?

  • Basic — 500 GB/Month (for basic usage, social media etc.)
  • Super — 1000 GB/Month 🔥 (for high-quality streaming)
  • Max — 2000 GB/Month (High speed, watch 4k videos without buffering, gaming, etc.)
  • Nano — 750 GB permanent data, 100 Mbps - no monthly reset, no device limit
  • Micro — 1500 GB permanent data, 100 Mbps - no monthly reset, no device limit
Note: You can either get Large Traffic/Data or High Speed, both can't be in the same package together.

These data limits are based on the usage (similar to using mobile data limit). Therefore if you use internet while being connected your package data will also be consumed based on your usage. This data limit will reset every month (except Nano & Micro which have permanent data).
